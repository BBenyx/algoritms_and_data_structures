## River crossing problem solver
  
#### The following people want to cross the river are:
* 2 weak people from team A
* 1 strong person from team A
* 2 weak person from team B
* 1 strong person from team B
* 1 killer
* 1 strong policeman
  
#### Rules we must follow:
* The boat can only hold 2 people at once
* Only a strong person can row
* If the policeman abandons the killer it will kill everybody on the same riverbank
* If a strong person form a team abandons its weak teammates on the while the strong person of the other team is present and the policeman is not then, the strong person from the other team will kill the abandoned weak people (who is not in its own team).
* Every body has to cross the river safe and sound.
